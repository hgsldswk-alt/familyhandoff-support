Help

Open one of the six cards, tap a common item to add it, then write a line on where it is and who to ask. We suggest writing where a password is kept rather than the password itself.

To hand the list to family, tap Hand it to family. Share a printable PDF, or send a locked file and tell them the 8-character passcode in person or by phone. They open the file in Family Handoff (Settings → Open a file from family) and enter the passcode; items are merged into their list.

Family Handoff is an organizer. It does not give legal or financial advice and does not replace a will.

Privacy

The app does not collect any data. Your list is stored only on your device, protected by iOS file encryption and, if you keep it on, Face ID. There is no account, server, advertising or analytics. PDFs and locked files are created on your device and only leave it when you share them yourself.

家人接手清单帮助

打开六组里的任意一组，点常见条目加进来，再补一句「在哪、找谁」。建议写密码放在哪，不必直接写密码。

交给家人：点「交给家人」，可以导出可打印的 PDF，或发送加密文件，并当面或电话告诉对方 8 位口令。对方在「家人接手清单」里（设置 → 打开家人发来的文件）输入口令打开，条目会合并进他的清单。

本 App 只是整理清单，不提供法律或理财建议，也不能代替遗嘱。

App 不收集任何数据。清单只保存在你的设备上，由 iOS 文件加密保护，开启时还需要 Face ID。没有账号、服务器、广告或统计。PDF 和加密文件在本机生成，只有你主动分享时才会离开设备。
